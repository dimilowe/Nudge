---
name: Nudg pricing policy
description: Creator subscription, trial, expiry and live Stripe business decisions.
---

Nudg offers one subscription for access to all of Nudg at $22 USD/month, with a 7-day free trial and no card required upfront. After the trial, payment is required to continue using the creator workspace. Published links remain live after subscription expiry; the workspace is locked to billing. Existing trials keep the end date they were originally given.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose one all-access plan, no-card trial and keeping public pages live, then changed the trial from 14 days to 7 days.

**How to apply:** Keep new-checkout pricing copy and server-side trial duration consistent; do not shorten trials already in progress. Do not reintroduce free beta or feature tiers. The user requested live Stripe setup instead of test mode; never perform a real customer payment on their behalf.
